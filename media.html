Why Urine Testing Matters

A urinalysis is a simple, non-invasive test that helps detect early signs of urinary tract infections, kidney disease, diabetes, dehydration, and other underlying conditions. Watch the video below to learn more about the process and what to expect during your visit.

What We Test For

  • Urinary Tract Infections (UTI)
  • Kidney function and disorders
  • Diabetes and blood sugar irregularities (glucose/ketones)
  • Liver function markers
  • Pregnancy screening
  • Hydration and general wellness indicators
